What I do

All services, and how an engagement works
01

Web applications

Full-stack products and internal dashboards with Next.js, React, TypeScript, and PostgreSQL: sign-in and role-based access, reporting and charts, admin tooling, and deployment to Google Cloud. Recent work: the analyst dashboard at AddressHate.

02

Internal tools and automation

Browser extensions, pipeline control panels, Jira and Confluence automation, and small desktop utilities. The kind of software that quietly saves a team hours every week. Recent work: a Chrome annotation extension that runs on 16 platforms, and an Atlassian plugin for producers at 2K.

03

Game development and engine tooling

Gameplay systems and editor tools in Unreal Engine 5 (C++) and Unity (C#): actor and item wizards, camera systems, ability frameworks, and the debug features that make testing faster. Three studio rotations at 2K Games, and a shipped title with a 30-person USC team.

04

Technical review and consulting

Architecture and code review, security hardening passes, and help scoping a build before you commit budget to it. Useful when you have an idea or a codebase and want a second set of eyes before the next step.

How I work

Scoped in writing

Every project starts with a short proposal and a statement of work: deliverables, milestones, and what's out of scope. No surprises on either side.

Fixed fee or hourly

Whichever fits the work. Fixed fees for well-defined builds, hourly for ongoing or exploratory work. Rates on request.

Your code, your accounts

Work lands in your repositories and your cloud projects from the first commit. You own everything I build for you.

Remote, on my own schedule and equipment

I work from Novato with my own tools. You get regular written updates, and a call whenever one is actually useful.

Have a project in mind?

Send a few sentences about what you're building, your timeline, and a budget range. I'll reply with questions or a short proposal.
