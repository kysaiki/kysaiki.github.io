What happens next

I read every inquiry myself and usually reply within a couple of business days. If the project sounds like a fit, the next step is a 30-minute call, then a written proposal and statement of work. Short engagements, such as a code review or a scoping session, can skip the proposal and start on an hourly basis.

Business details

Kyler Saiki is an independent software engineering business, operated as a sole proprietorship by Kyler Saiki in Novato, California, and registered with the City of Novato. Established June 2026.

I work remotely on my own equipment and schedule, set my own rates, and serve more than one client at a time. Every engagement runs under a written agreement or statement of work, and invoices are issued under my own name. A W-9 is available on request.

Services: web application development, internal tools and automation, game development and engine tooling, and technical review. See Services for detail.
